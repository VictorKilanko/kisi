# Instagram captions

One caption per image, numbered to match `images/NN-*.png`. Copy the caption, attach
the matching PNG, post. A reusable hashtag block is at the bottom; paste it under any
caption (Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags, but 10 to 15 relevant ones perform best).

**Before you post:** the images show the website address `kisi.africa`. Your Instagram
**@handle** is not baked into the images, so set it once here. Suggested: **@kisi.africa**
(confirm it is available and yours). Replace it below if you use a different handle.

Recommended order: post 01 to 12 in sequence. The three leader posts (04 to 06) work
as a single carousel. The five election posts (08 to 12) are a serial: post one every
day or two, or release them together as one carousel titled "The Nesting Box Election."

---

## 01 · Welcome to Kisi Farm
Welcome to Kisi Farm: a working poultry farm in southwestern Nigeria, run by the chickens
themselves. They hold the offices. They keep the records. They run the whole place like
their own little republic. Taco greets you first, so come in. This is where every chicken
has a story, and we are just getting started. Follow along. 🐔🇳🇬

## 02 · Where every chicken has a story
Every hen here has a name, a history, and a job. Some run ministries. One runs the whole
Republic. None of them is inventory, and every one of them is worth meeting. Over the
coming weeks we will introduce the leaders, the news, and the stories from Kisi Farm.
Who do you want to meet first? 👇

## 03 · Meet Taco, the mascot
Meet Taco. No office. All heart. Outranks everybody anyway. She is first to the gate,
first to every gathering, and first to welcome a face she has never seen. Her whole
philosophy: "You don't need a title to show up first." Say hello to Taco below. 🧡

## 04 · President Adédoyin "Mama Decree"
Meet the First Citizen: President Adédoyin, known to all as Mama Decree. She rose from an
ordinary nest in Coop Three to the highest perch in the land on the slogan "Forward with
Feed." Her first act in office was Executive Order No. 1: breakfast at seven. Not
seven-ish. As she puts it, "A nation that eats late, lays late." 👑

## 05 · Halima "Iron Feathers", Leader of the Opposition
Meet the Republic's auditor-in-chief by sheer force of personality: Halima "Iron
Feathers," Leader of the Opposition and founder of the New Grain Alliance. Her monthly
demand for a recount of the National Egg Census is now part of the calendar, like the
rains. "We will count every egg. Twice. And if the totals agree, we count a third time,
because agreement is suspicious." 🧮

## 06 · Chief Justice Yèyé Àlàbá
Meet the Settler of Perches: Chief Justice Yèyé Àlàbá of the Supreme Pecking Council. She
hears every case in the shade after the afternoon feed, on the principle that no bird is
reasonable while hungry. She rules in proverbs, and she is never appealed. Every session
ends the same way: "Court is risen. Go and share something." ⚖️

## 07 · Every chair filled
Breaking from The Coop Times: in a single week, President Adédoyin filled six empty
cabinet seats and retired the word "acting" from the national vocabulary. The Opposition
welcomed the new ministers warmly, and promised to audit all six budgets by Friday. Read
the full story at kisi.africa/news. 🗞️

## 08 · The Nesting Box Election, Part 1 of 5
THE NESTING BOX ELECTION begins. "Forward with Feed" meets "Count Every Egg. Twice."
Posters go up on every post. Someone draws a moustache on the President's poster. It is,
inevitably, investigated. Follow this account to see how the Republic votes. Part 1 of 5. 🗳️

## 09 · The Nesting Box Election, Part 2 of 5
Part 2 of 5. Neither big party can pass the Nesting Box Bill without the Free Feathers
Front, the small party that meets under the mango tree and opens with one minute of
contented scratching. Overnight, the least hurried birds in the Republic become the most
courted. 🌳

## 10 · The Nesting Box Election, Part 3 of 5
Part 3 of 5. Voting day, under the mango tree. Chief Justice Yèyé Àlàbá presides with a
single rule: no bird votes hungry. Voting closes at the afternoon feed. Turnout is
declared total, because she counted everyone on the way in. 🐓

## 11 · The Nesting Box Election, Part 4 of 5
Part 4 of 5. The New Grain Alliance demands a recount before the first count has even
finished, on principle. The Bureau of Egg Statistics obliges. The totals agree, which the
Alliance finds deeply suspicious, and says so. 📊

## 12 · The Nesting Box Election, Part 5 of 5
Part 5 of 5, the finale. The Progressive Peckers keep their majority; the Free Feathers
Front trades its support for a real expansion of nesting boxes. That evening the President
and the Opposition Leader are seen at the fence line, arguing about everything except the
result, which is already settled between them. Read the whole arc at
kisi.africa/republic/stories. 🤝
