Where the budget comes from

The plainest thing in Kisi: the whole Republic runs on eggs. The feed that keeps the hens laying is the first line of the budget, not the one to trim. Every egg that leaves the gate pays for tomorrow’s grain. Order farm-fresh eggs at farm.kisi.africa/eggs.
