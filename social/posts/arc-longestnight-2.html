The volunteer

Small Fẹ́mi, last of the twelve out of Coop Two on the night of the Drain, offered to keep watch through the dark hours. ‘I’d rather sit in a true dark than pretend it isn’t there.’ Baba Ṣẹ́gun, who shares the dawn crow with him: ‘You won’t sit alone. I’ll crow the watches, you walk them.’
