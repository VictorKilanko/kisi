The move at last

For four seasons Halima fought the President as loyal opposition, then made peace at the fence each dusk. This morning she rose cold as iron and moved for the first chair itself, and meant it. In the gallery Sweet Beak could barely sit still: the two that could not be split had split, in front of everyone.
