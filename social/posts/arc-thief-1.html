A sweet little question

Ládùn wanted a ministry. After four schemes the flock laughed off, she stopped whispering and went for proof. She watched Tadé carry the chicks’ leftovers past the water tank, as he does so none is wasted. ‘Where does all that feed go?’ she asked, sweetly.
