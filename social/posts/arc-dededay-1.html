When is Dede’s?

Zizi asks forty questions a day, and this one stopped the yard. Her uncle Dede is first to every hatch-day in Kisi: first to the gate, first to sing, first to turn up uninvited. So when is his own? Nobody knew. He has no records. He just turned up one morning and never left.
