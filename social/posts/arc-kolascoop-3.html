The shift that is never news

By morning Kola had his story, and not the one he came for. These birds are only news when they fail, and they spend every night making sure they never are. He printed it as the plainest tribute he knew, under the headline THE SHIFT THAT IS NEVER NEWS. Danladi read it once, said it was too kind, and went back on duty.
