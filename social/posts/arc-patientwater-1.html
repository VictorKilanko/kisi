The happiest bored minister

In the dry season Emeka the Drain Marshal has little to do, and it is the joy of his year. A bored water minister means nothing has gone wrong: troughs full, water clean. ‘I would rather be bored in the dry season,’ he says, ‘than busy in a flood.’
