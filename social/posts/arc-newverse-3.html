The verse that changes tempo

Then she did what only a griot can: instead of choosing a tempo, she wrote one that moves. Slow and grand to open, on Baba’s dawn note. Quick for the young. Wide and easy for the elders. Everyone in it, nobody made to lose. That dusk the whole flock sang it together.
