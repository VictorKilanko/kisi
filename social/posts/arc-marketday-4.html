What leaves Kisi

The President came to see the crates off and made no decree, only: ‘Everything else we argue about. This we agree on: good eggs, counted true, sent with our name on them.’ Kola Quill printed his gentlest headline of the year: THE EGGS ARE GOOD. Order farm-fresh eggs at farm.kisi.africa/eggs.
