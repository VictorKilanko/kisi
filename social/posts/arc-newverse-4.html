What the song is about

And the verse? The plainest thing in the Republic: the working hens who rise every morning and lay. No decree runs without them, and no song either. ‘The singing is free,’ said Sadé. ‘The grain is not.’ Order farm-fresh eggs at farm.kisi.africa/eggs.
