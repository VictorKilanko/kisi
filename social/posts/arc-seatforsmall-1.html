Tadé moves the bill

Tadé the Foraging Master has argued one thing longest: the young deserve a real voice, not a pat on the head. With the new clutch barely off the heat lamp, he moved the Young Chick Representation Bill, asking that one young bird get a seat where decisions are made. What does a chick know about the budget, some asked.
