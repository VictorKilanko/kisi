The First Rains

Part 1 of 4

The sky changes

After the long dry season the air went heavy and the first real storm built over the far fence. There was a time this meant a flooded yard and a cold, dangerous night. Emeka the Drain Marshal read the sky, checked his channels and the barred drain, and did what old Kisi could not. He told the flock to let it come.
