The next target

Sweet Beak never forgot the front page: ‘SHE TOLD BOTH,’ read by the whole Republic over breakfast. You cannot out-whisper a printed word, so she picked a new target, the newsman himself. Make the flock stop trusting Kola Quill, and they’d stop trusting the bird who caught her.
