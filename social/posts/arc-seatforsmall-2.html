A Seat for the Small

Part 2 of 4

A seat is a responsibility

Halima rose against making it easy. A seat is a weight, she said, not a gift, and a bird too young to carry it is set up to fail so the rest of us feel warm. It read as cold. It was just Halima, counting the cost first. So they set the honest test: let a young bird stand and show which it would be.
