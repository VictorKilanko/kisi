The Saturday nobody sees

Every Saturday young Small Fẹ́mi walks the fence ‘where nobody can see him,’ and everybody can. He wants to be a coop guard; Danladi keeps saying: finish growing first. But the Sergeant does the dusk whistle every night, and he’d seen the boy out in the cold.
