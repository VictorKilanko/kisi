The First Chair

Part 4 of 4

Iron, tempered

The first chair was one word away, and part of Halima still wanted it. For one breath, even she didn’t know. Then she withdrew: ‘I won’t let a schemer split two crate-sisters.’ The bond came back tempered, like iron from fire. We count every egg by hand. Order yours at farm.kisi.africa/eggs.
