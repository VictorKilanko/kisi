A clean morning

First light found the yard already drained, the troughs full of the cleanest water of the year, and a flock that slept through a storm that would once have been a disaster. Under the mango tree Mama Gold, who has seen every rainy season, said she could remember when the first rains were something you survived, not something you woke up grateful for.
