Market Day

Counted honest, sold honest

Mama Gold presided over the loading the way she does everything now: slowly, with her whole heart. Every egg was counted by hand and counted true. ‘A market only comes back,’ she said, ‘to a farm that does not lie to it.’ The buyers pay for the egg and return for the honesty.
