A story that isn’t a scandal

After a long run of schemers and apology tours, Kola Quill was tired of noise. The loudest stories, he felt, were rarely the most important, and the important ones hide in the minutes nobody reads. So he asked a question his paper had never run: who keeps Kisi alive while it sleeps? Nobody had written it, because nothing goes wrong.
