The layers eat first

Musa said it plainly: ‘The laying hens eat first.’ Not the champions, not the chicks, not even the elders he loves. The league, the school, the bench, all of it is paid for by what the hens lay. A hen not fed does not lay. Halima checked the numbers. ‘Noted.’
