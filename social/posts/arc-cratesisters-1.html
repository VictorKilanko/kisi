The Crate-Sisters

Part 1 of 4

At the fence, in daylight

For years the Republic’s two fiercest rivals met at the fence only at dusk, where nobody could see. President Adédoyin and Halima arrived in the same chick crate; the friendship under the feud was the worst-kept secret in Kisi. Then Kola saw them scratching side by side in broad daylight, not hiding.
