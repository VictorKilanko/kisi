Sweet Beak’s Sixth Try

Part 1 of 4

Five misses, one idea

Ládùn Sweet Beak has tried to talk her way into a ministry five times, and missed five times. This time, she decided, the trouble was her targets: she kept picking birds the flock could defend. So she picked the opposite, and landed by accident on the hardest target in Kisi. Quiet Grace.
