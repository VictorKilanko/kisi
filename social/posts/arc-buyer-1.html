The Buyer Who Came Back

Part 1 of 4

Twice what we sold

The first buyer ever to take Kisi eggs to market came back, basket bigger. Last season he took one crate. Now he wanted two, standing, every week, starting today. The whole farm heard the number and stopped. Two crates was not what the hens had actually laid.
