The question nobody asked

Tadé sent Small Fẹ́mi, the Drain survivor who prefers a true answer to a kind one. He made no speech. He asked the plain question the Assembly had stepped around for weeks: the new clutch will be grown by the next dry season. Will their coop be rebuilt to Cindy’s Law by then, or are we only hoping? The room went quiet.
