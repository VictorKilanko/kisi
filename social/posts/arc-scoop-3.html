The minutes nobody reads

‘Too good’ has never fooled Kola Quill. He nearly ran it, headline drafted, press warming. Then his old habit stopped him: he checks. The memo fell apart against the real ledger, and the scratch-marks were in a sweet, looping hand he already knew. ‘The minutes nobody reads are the story everybody needs.’
