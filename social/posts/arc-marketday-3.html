Chi-Chi’s first crate

For Chi-Chi it was a first. She came to Kisi motherless at three weeks old, and a whole nation once waited on her first egg. Now her eggs went to market for the very first time. She wondered if plain brown eggs like hers were good enough. Then the crate closed over them all together. ‘I did my best.’
