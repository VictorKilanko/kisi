# Curriculum · Songs

**Simple, joyful songs** kids can sing along to. Age 2-7. Format: a lyrics page with a lively
illustrated layout now; **real audio comes later** (recorded/AI, then embedded).

## Rules
- **Original songs** written for Kisi Kids are fine (mark as original). **Traditional songs** must
  have their origin noted and be genuinely traditional/public-domain; do not misattribute.
- Values-positive, repetitive, easy to remember. Short.

## Format (per song, one page + one future video)
- Title, a short intro from Zizi, the lyrics (with a simple repeated chorus), and a note of
  origin (original vs traditional).

## Seed set
- **"Good Morning, Kisi"** (original) — a gentle counting/greeting wake-up song; Dede and Zizi
  greet the farm, count the sunrise things. Teaches counting 1-5 + a warm greeting. [SEED, lyrics
  only this build; audio later]
- A traditional lullaby or counting song (note the tradition) — added when verified.
