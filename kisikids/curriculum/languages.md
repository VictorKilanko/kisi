# Curriculum · Languages (Word of the Day)

**Real African words and greetings, taught accurately.** Age 3-8. Format: a "word of the day"
card, the word + meaning + how to say it, with Zizi practising and Dede confirming.

## Rules
- **Accuracy first.** Spelling, meaning, and pronunciation must be correct; note the language.
  Start with languages we can get right (Yoruba, Swahili). When unsure of a word or its tone
  marks, leave it out rather than guess.
- Respect the language: real greetings and everyday words, not a caricature.

## Format (per word, one card + one future video segment)
- The word (with tone marks where needed), the language, the meaning, a simple pronunciation
  ("say it like ..."), and a one-line use ("say it to greet a friend in the morning").

## Seed set
- **Ẹ káàárọ̀** (Yoruba) — "Good morning." Say: "eh KAA-ah-raw". [SEED for this build]
- **Jambo** (Swahili) — "Hello."
- **Ẹ ṣé** (Yoruba) — "Thank you."
- **Asante** (Swahili) — "Thank you."

Verify each word's spelling/tone before adding. Keep to a few high-confidence words per language.
