# Curriculum · African History

**True African history, told simply and with pride.** Age 5-8. Format: a short illustrated
"did you know?" story, framed by Zizi's curiosity and Dede's steady telling.

## Rules
- **Real and accurate.** Places, rough eras, and achievements must be correct; simplify without
  distorting. Non-partisan. Celebrate genuine African achievement and ingenuity.
- Note the source basis in the content record. When a detail is uncertain, leave it out.

## Format (per topic, one page + one future video)
- A single, child-simple idea: a kingdom, a place, an invention, a way of life.
- What it was, roughly when/where, and one thing that makes a child go "wow".

## Seed ideas (verify facts before building each)
- The great learning city of **Timbuktu** (Mali) — books and scholars, long ago.
- The **Great Mosque of Djenné** (Mali) — the largest mud-brick building in the world.
- **Great Zimbabwe** — a huge stone city built without mortar.
- Ancient **Nubia / Kush** and its pyramids.

Start with one, done carefully, when this pillar is built out. Facts over flourish.
