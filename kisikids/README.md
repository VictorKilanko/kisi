# kisikids/

The planning + content brain for **Kisi Kids** (kids.kisi.africa), the children's brand of the
Kisi universe, hosted by **Dede** (the warm uncle) and **Zizi** (his curious young niece).

**To continue development:** read **`KIDS_PLAN.md`** and start at its **▶ RESUME HERE** marker.

- `KIDS_PLAN.md` — the runbook: vision, pillars, roadmap, resume marker.
- `BIBLE.md` — Dede + Zizi: voices, look, tone, the two hard rules (real facts; child-safe).
- `PIPELINE.md` — the AI video pipeline (for when video tools are ready).
- `curriculum/` — outlines per pillar (history, fables, songs, languages, heroes).
- `scripts/` — episode scripts (`TEMPLATE.md` is the format).
- `assets/` — where real art and audio land (see its README).

The website lives in `apps/kids/`; the cast lives in `packages/canon` (Zizi = `id: "zizi"`).
