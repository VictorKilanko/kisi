# kisikids/assets — art & audio drop area

Put finished Kisi Kids assets here. These override the interim procedural SVG art on the site and
seed the video pipeline. Keep everything consistent with `../BIBLE.md`.

## What lands here
- **Model sheets (priority):** `model-sheet-dede.*`, `model-sheet-zizi.*` — the definitive
  character reference (turnarounds, expressions, the canon colours). Every video seeds from these.
- **Character art:** reusable Dede/Zizi illustrations (poses, expressions).
- **Scene / story illustrations:** per fable/history page, if commissioned to replace the SVG art.
- **Song audio:** `song-<slug>.mp3` once recorded (original) or licensed (traditional, with proof).
- **Printable art:** high-res line art for colouring pages, if replacing the SVG printables.

## Rules
- Match the character colours and shapes in the bible so nothing drifts.
- Traditional songs/fables: keep a note of origin / licence here alongside the file.
- No stock or unlicensed assets. Keep source files where possible.

Until real art arrives, the site uses `@kisi/ui` `ChickenPortrait` (from each bird's canon colours)
and procedural SVG scenes, which are deterministic and consistent.
